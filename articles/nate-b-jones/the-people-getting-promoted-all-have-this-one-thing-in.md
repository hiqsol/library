## The Career Ladder Is Being Disassembled

Entry-level hiring at major tech companies has collapsed by more than 50%
since 2019. Entry-level job postings across the US economy declined by 29
percentage points between January 2024 and January 2026. The unemployment
rate for recent college grads now exceeds the broader unemployment rate.

The traditional career ladder — working at a brand-name tech company, doing
your time, delivering competent results, climbing from individual contributor
to manager to director to VP — is being disassembled while people are still
standing on it. This is not a temporary hiring freeze. It is a fundamental
restructuring of how careers work.

The routine tasks that once trained newcomers — summarizing meetings,
cleaning data, drafting memos, processing information — are precisely the
tasks that generative AI handles with increasing competence. Entry-level
jobs today require experience that entry-level roles no longer supply. AI
has cannibalized the low-risk work that used to teach people how to operate
in complex organizations.

## High Agency Is the Answer

When the ladder disappears, the answer is high agency — not ordinary high
agency, but off-the-charts, paradigm-altering high agency.

High agency is not a feeling. It is not a sense of empowerment or
confidence. It is better understood through a concept that psychologist
Julian Rotter identified in the 1950s: **locus of control**.

### The Circle Exercise

Take a piece of paper. Draw a circle. Write down all the major elements in
your life that matter to you: family, friends, business, projects,
education, your next promotion, your goals. Whatever falls inside the
circle is what you perceive as under your control. Whatever falls outside
is what you perceive as beyond your influence.

- **Low agency people:** More significant elements end up outside the
  circle. The next promotion — that's up to the manager. The learning
  required — no time. Major life changes — the economy, circumstances
  beyond my control.
- **High agency people:** Everything is inside the circle. Everything.
  Promotion, compensation, learning curve, location, skill development,
  family goals, career goals — all inside, without exception.

When high agency people hear that inner voice suggesting something is beyond
their control, they respond with four words: **"That's a skill issue."**

## Addressing the Objections

This is not bootstrap ideology. AI represents a jet engine on the back of
high agency people. Several important nuances:

- You do not literally control whether a layoff happens to you. But having
  the mindset that your next step is always within your control is both
  realistic and statistically correlated with better life trajectories.
- People from historically disadvantaged backgrounds who succeed tend to
  have even higher agency — not because the deck is fair, but because when
  you have less margin for error, the behavioral trait becomes even more
  essential.
- Excessive internality can lead to anxiety. The ask is not to blame
  yourself for every setback. The ask is to channel that internal
  orientation into challenge and opportunity.

There is a difference between "I failed because I'm inadequate" and
"I haven't figured this out yet, but I can work with AI to figure it out."

## AI Is the Greatest Equalizer for Agency

People have gone from dead-end jobs and hopeless situations to running
their own businesses — not because they got lucky, but because they took
a high agency mindset, used AI to learn what they did not know, and
relentlessly executed.

The barriers that used to require years of expensive education or access
to the right networks can now be overcome by someone with a laptop and
determination. AI does not care about your pedigree. It responds to your
questions.

## The Forcing Function

Before this moment, whether you had high agency or not sorted itself out
over decades. The signal was gradual. You could coast.

Because AI acts as such a powerful accelerant, people with high agency
are now accomplishing 10x, 100x, 1000x more than people with low agency.
The gap that once took 20 years to emerge now happens in months. Career
stagnation that would have taken a decade to become apparent now manifests
in a year or two.

## The Research on Internal Locus of Control

People with an internal locus of control show:

- Greater persistence when facing challenges
- Higher academic achievement
- Better professional outcomes
- More effective leadership
- Stronger entrepreneurial activity

Students with an internal locus of control outperform those who attribute
outcomes to external forces by 20–30% on average. Companies led by CEOs
with an internal locus of control are more likely to survive difficult
periods.

## The Say-Do Ratio

If you say you are going to do something, do it. Not tomorrow. Not after
making a plan. Now.

Most people have a terrible say-do ratio. They say they will learn a new
skill and then research courses for three weeks. They say they will start
a side project and spend a month choosing the perfect tech stack.

There is extraordinary progress available when you collapse the distance
between saying you will do something and actually doing it — starting when
it feels uncomfortable, beginning before you feel ready, shipping halfway
done rather than waiting for perfection.

## High Agency Transforms How You Use AI

The breathless articles about students using AI as an answer engine, about
losing critical thinking — those outcomes happen when people are passive.
A high agency approach treats AI as an extension of your own capacity:
exploring problems more deeply, learning skills faster, prototyping ideas.

## Solo Founders and the New Reality

- Solo founders in VC-backed startups rose from 22% in 2015 to 38% in 2024
- Dario Amodei (CEO of Anthropic) predicts the first one-person $1B
  company this year; Sam Altman says 2028
- AI unicorns reached billion-dollar valuations with ~200 employees;
  some now have ~20
- Ma'or Shlomo built Base44 from a side project to an $80M acquisition
  by Wix in six months — no venture, no full-time team, pushing to
  production 13 times a day, generating $200K/month in profit by May 2025

## Value Creation as the Animating Purpose

High agency people are obsessed with pushing value into the world. They
know that if the value they generate is high enough, it will come back
to them. The primary orientation is toward contribution rather than
extraction.

High agency people believe the world is bendable. It will bend in the
direction you want if you push enough value out and invest enough in
expanding your locus of control.

## Practical Steps

1. Identify a goal you would traditionally perceive as outside your
   control
2. Ask what it would take to bring that goal inside your locus of control
3. Ask what you would need to learn
4. Use AI to map the path and accelerate through it
5. Have an honest conversation: "What would a person with an extremely
   high internal locus of control do in my situation?"
6. Do that thing today — not after more research

The future belongs to those who understand this and act on it. Not
eventually. Not after making a plan. Now.
