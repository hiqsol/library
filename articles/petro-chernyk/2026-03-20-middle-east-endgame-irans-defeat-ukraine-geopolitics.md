- *15,000+ targets* struck in Iran since the operation began
- Iranian rocket activity down *90%*, drone use down *95%*
- Khark Island struck: 90+ Iranian military objects destroyed, including naval mine and missile

estimate: ~2,000 heavy rockets, ~500 launch systems. Iran built into mountainous terrain over
decades — tunnels, retractable mountain silos. Complete elimination is genuinely difficult.

## The "Stockholm Syndrome" Warning

Chernyk's central and most emphatic point: Ukrainian information space is being contaminated with
sympathy toward Iran. He calls this *"Stockholm syndrome toward the Iranian node"* and identifies
it as dangerous to the point of being criminal.

The Russian-planted narrative: *"The US attack on Iran is a special military operation — the same
as Russia's against Ukraine."* This is a deliberate disinformation operation. Chernyk's rebuttal:

*The two theaters — Russia-Ukraine and US-Iran — are one geopolitical vector, not two.* Both
are democracies fighting tyrannies that attacked them first. The Iranian regime *directly
participated in the genocide of Ukrainians* — Shahed-136 drones killed thousands of Ukrainians,
destroyed tens of thousands of homes, ruined millions of lives. Regular Iranian citizens operated
those assembly lines and loading docks, and they knew exactly what they were doing.

Historical parallel: After WWII, denazification applied to *all Germans*, not just Nazi party
members. The healthy, non-Nazi generation emerged only in the 1980s. The Iranian regime must be
held accountable — and the distinction between "regime" and "people" doesn't erase collective
responsibility when millions participated in producing that war machine.

## Military Analysis: Iran's Remaining Capabilities

*Rockets:* Real degradation, but not total. Mountain terrain + underground infrastructure means
the US is striking from sea and European bases (B-52s). Afghanistan withdrawal in 2021 was a
strategic mistake — that base would have enabled far more systematic destruction of Iran's
missile program from the east.

*Ormuz Strait:* To truly secure navigation, a large naval presence is needed, not just air
strikes on Khark Island. The US is now only *2% dependent* on Persian Gulf oil — China depends
90%, Japan significantly, Europe 15-20%. This is why Trump is pushing NATO allies to patrol
Ormuz themselves. Six countries confirmed: UK, France, Germany, Italy, Netherlands, Japan.

*US amphibious capability:* Chernyk rejects the narrative that the US lacks capacity for
amphibious operations. ~30 heavy universal landing ships, plus V-22 Osprey convertiplane,
Super Stallion helicopters, AAV-P7 amphibious APCs. Could land ~10,000 Marines in a first wave.
Khark Island is 25 km from the mainland — a landing would require an inland bridgehead.
US is currently operating at *less than 5% of its real capacity.*

*Nuclear plant at Bushehr:* Chernyk notes this as another potential raid target.

## Europe: The Reluctant Partner

Chernyk is blunt: Europe lived under the American umbrella for 80 years and developed deep
introverted inertia. The narrative that "Europeans were right to resist American pressure" is
wrong. Tyrannies haven't disappeared. The real global confrontation is coming regardless.

*The stakes:* If Europe doesn't consolidate and show teeth, the Caliphate stops in Glasgow and
Stockholm. Historical precedent: the Córdoba Caliphate occupied Iberia for 720 years. Ottoman
Islam reached Vienna — only the Holy League expelled it. There is no alternative to
consolidation.

*What Europe has but won't share with Ukraine:*
- At least *1,500 operational fighters* (Gripen, Rafale, Mirage 2000, Tornado, Eurofighter)
- At least *4,000 Leopard 1 + Leopard 2* platforms
Spain scrapped dozens of working M113s for scrap metal last year rather than give them to
Ukraine. This is the reality.

*Russia and the Baltics:* Chernyk does not agree that Russia can't attack Baltic states due to
lack of equipment. A 40,000-strong corps armed with Kalashnikovs (millions still in warehouses —
100M+ produced) could trigger NATO Article 5, but Hungary and Slovakia would likely veto it. He
cites British General Richard Shirreff's book *War with Russia*.

## China: Playing Both Sides

China's economy is *80% export-oriented* toward the Euro-Atlantic world. It needs Persian Gulf
oil — 90% of its supply passes through Hormuz. Real Chinese material support to Iran has been
near zero: no confirmed shipments of weapons systems, no Chinese contingent.

Historical parallel: In the Soviet-Afghan war, *90% of mujahideen weapons through 1986 were
Chinese* — purchased with Saudi money ($200-300M/year), including 70,000 tonnes of ammunition in
one year, plus 600 Chinese specialists in Pakistan. China knows how to play covert proxy games
and chose *not to* this time.

Why? Because the US hasn't yet targeted Iranian oil terminals. China is calculating: *what
happens if America really turns on the taps?* That is the deterrent preventing a Chinese "Lend-
Lease for Iran."

Trump's invitation to China to co-patrol Ormuz is partly a genuine offer and partly a trap —
either China helps stabilize its own oil supply, or it exposes itself as an obstacle.

## Dubai and the Russian Money Laundering Network

An unexpected data point: large numbers of pets (cats and dogs) abandoned on streets of Dubai
and other Gulf cities after Iranian strikes. Chernyk's reading: the pets belonged to *wealthy
Russians*, who are now fleeing.

The Gulf has been a *grey money laundering center for decades* — narco-trafficking connected to
Afghanistan, and Russian sanctions evasion: microelectronics, components, dual-use materials for
the Russian military-industrial complex. The X-47M2 Kinzhal missile contains *130 components of
Western manufacture* — that supply chain ran through the Gulf.

Dubai received *$140 billion in investment in 2024*. That capital base is now disrupted. Russian
grey procurement networks are weakened — not eliminated, but significantly constrained.

## The United States Is Not Falling

Chernyk dismisses the narrative of American decline. The US absorbed *$1.5 trillion in net
investment inflows last year*, including $80 billion from Europeans who understand where real
financial stability lies. The US can only fall under three conditions: a large nuclear strike,
internal separatism, or the Yellowstone supervolcano. None of these is happening.

The hegemon remembered it was the hegemon and is returning to the world stage. Chernyk's
position: *better American hegemony than Chinese-Russian hegemony.* This is not a choice between
good and bad — it is a choice between acceptable and unacceptable.

## Energy Revolution Ahead

Chernyk's long-term view: the world is on the threshold of a major energy revolution. Dirty fuel
(oil) will be phased out over decades, not years. The coming revolution will be *nuclear* —
specifically helium-3. The US return to the Moon is not symbolic. These are the early moves of
a civilization-scale energy transition. Cites Peter Zeihan's *The End of the World Is Just the
Beginning* as essential reading.
