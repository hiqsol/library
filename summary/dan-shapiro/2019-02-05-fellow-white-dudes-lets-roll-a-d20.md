# Summary: Fellow White Dudes: Let's Roll a d20

**Author:** Dan Shapiro
**Published:** 2019-02-05 14:17

Last week, I attended an unconference called ORD Camp. At the event, attendees create sessions
together around their passions and expertise.

The organizers, Zach and Fitz, have been deliberate about inviting diverse participants. This year,
they called me up and asked me to give a short introductory talk.

## Key Points

- Last week, I attended an unconference called ORD Camp. At the event, attendees create sessions
  together around their passions and expertise.
- The organizers, Zach and Fitz, have been deliberate about inviting diverse participants. This
  year, they called me up and asked me to give a short introductory talk.
- Hi! I'm a white dude.
- I'll be honest - I'm terrified. Talking about race and gender is deeply uncomfortable for me.
- As white dudes, I think we share some things in common.
- We're usually surrounded by lots of other white dudes. That's significant, because humans feel
  more comfortable when we see people who look like us.
- We're raised in a society where it's unusual that speaking up puts us in danger. Most of us don't
  regularly stop to think - if I say this, am I putting myself in jeopardy?
- We're rewarded and encouraged for making our voices heard. Society expects us to take up a lot of
  room in the room, and many of us are raised to do that.
