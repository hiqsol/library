# Summary: THE 10 FOREVER RULES OF AI

Dan Shapiro revisits ten AI principles originally drafted in June 2023, comparing them against the
reality of late 2025. The core theme is that while the technology moves with blinding speed, the
strategic constraints and incentives for builders remain remarkably consistent.

## Key Strategic Rules:

*   **Avoid Chasing SOTA Benchmarks:** Foundation models improve faster than individual innovators
    can optimize current versions. Focus on building value rather than squeezing performance from a
    specific model version.
*   **Embrace Model Agnosticism:** Leadership among models (e.g., GPT-5-Codex, Opus, Gemini 2.5 Pro)
    is transient. Avoid lock-in to any single provider or technology stack.
*   **Hosted Models Prevail:** Due to GPU scarcity and closed-source advantages, the most capable
    models will continue to be hosted services rather than self-hosted solutions.
*   **The Persistence of RAG:** Despite massive increases in context windows (up to 10M tokens),
    real-world data will always exceed model memory, ensuring Retrieval-Augmented Generation remains
    a permanent architectural requirement.
*   **Truth Over Tokens:** Validated, human-verified "ground-truth" data is the most durable
    strategic asset. LLMs identify patterns, but facts must be anchored externally to prevent
    "garbage-in, garbage-out" cycles.
*   **Minimize Commitments:** In an era of weekly knowledge expiration, long-term financial or deep
    integration bets on specific third-party AI services are high-risk. Flexibility is the most
    valuable trait for AI-driven organizations.

The article underscores that "AI Knowledge" decays rapidly, necessitating a much more aggressive
tracking of the field than is standard for traditional software engineering. Despite the
volatility, Shapiro bets that these core principles will hold for at least another two years.
