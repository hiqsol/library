**Author:** Dan Shapiro

- The Turnigy Aerodrive SK3 - 2118-3100kv motor will let it fly straight up... while accelerating.

- Props have two numbers. The first is diameter, which gives you lifting power.
- The aforementioned motor is actually a brushless AC motor (thanks, Nicola ) so you'll need a
  "speed controller" to convert your DC battery to AC pulses that can run it. The H-King 10A ESC is
  a fine choice for the job ($6.75).
