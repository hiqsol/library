# Summary: How to succeed at Kickstarter

I've been asked by a lot of folks for advice with their Kickstarter campaigns. I've thought long and
hard about it.

This was a blog post written in the middle of the storm. I wrote up some of the techniques I used to
construct the campaign and published some live data from the actual campaign, including traffic
sources and amounts.
