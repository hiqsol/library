# The Skill That Separates AI Power Users From Everyone Else — Summary

[article](../../articles/nate-b-jones/2026-01-21-the-skill-that-separates-ai-power-users-from-everyone-else.md)

Cursor CEO Michael Truell ran GPT-5.2 for a week straight (Jan 2026),
producing 3 million lines of Rust code — a functional browser rendering
engine. This forces the question: is your AI colleague-shaped or
tool-shaped?

Claude Code (Anthropic, Feb 2025) is colleague-shaped: fast feedback
cycles, clarifying questions, iterative dialogue. Originally completed
45+ min tasks; now stretches to a full day of work. Designed around
human-in-the-loop collaboration.

Codex (OpenAI) is tool-shaped: autonomous execution from spec to
completion, running in cloud sandboxes for hours or days without human
intervention. CNC machine metaphor — executes precisely but faithfully
reproduces wrong specs.

Senior engineers report doubled PR output with Codex because they have
the domain expertise to write precise specs. GPT-5.2 outperforms even
the coding-specific GPT-5.1 Codex on long-horizon tasks — raw reasoning
beats narrow training.

Junior/mid-level developers benefit more from Claude Code's scaffolding.
The iterative dialogue teaches while building. Anthropic survey of ~130
engineers: most can fully delegate only ~20% of work to Claude Code.

Cursor's multi-agent experiment: hundreds of agents collaborating,
hierarchical planner/worker/reviewer structure. Estimated 3B+ tokens
consumed. Result was alpha-quality, not production.

The philosophical split: OpenAI sees the human as overhead to minimize
(spec then execution). Anthropic sees the back-and-forth as the
mechanism through which good work gets made.

For non-technical work, the question of what a high-quality spec looks
like is almost entirely unexplored — one of the big questions of 2026.

Key insight: most people overestimate their ability to specify precise
intent. The feedback loop that makes Claude Code feel slower is the
mechanism that sharpens intent.

Competitive advantage comes from developing high-quality intent
specification skills across your organization to leverage both
colleague-shaped and tool-shaped AI.
