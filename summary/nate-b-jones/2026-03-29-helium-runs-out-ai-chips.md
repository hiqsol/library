# Summary

[article](../../articles/nate-b-jones/2026-03-29-helium-runs-out-ai-chips.md)

Qatar's Ras Laffan complex — 33% of world helium supply — has been
damaged by missile strikes and is offline. Helium is irreplaceable
in chip manufacturing (EUV lithography, plasma etching, leak
testing). Liquid helium containers last only 35–48 days before
vaporizing. This threatens the physical substrate of AI.

## Key Numbers

- 2.4B standard cubic feet/year from Qatar (33% of 7B global)
- 14% of Qatar helium permanently damaged, 5-year reconstruction
- 300mm EUV fabs: 5,000–20,000 m³ helium/month at 6N purity
- SK Hynix: 2/3 of helium from Qatar
- TSMC: 11 days LNG reserves, imports 97% of energy
- Helium spot prices doubled, contract surcharges up 30%
- DRAM prices already up 70%, HBM already sold out
- Memory costs elevated through at least mid-2027

## Three Impact Channels

1. **Helium → chips** — SK Hynix and Samsung (world's largest
   memory makers) directly exposed. HBM for every AI accelerator.
2. **LNG → energy costs** — East Asian and European fabs face
   higher input costs for years.
3. **Geopolitics** — China gains advantage via domestic helium
   (Guangdong plant, 6N certified) and Power of Siberia 2 pipeline
   for cheap Russian LNG.

## Bottom Line

Fabs won't shut down but will run at lower capacity with higher
costs. US data centers are chip-exposed (supplied by Taiwan/Korea).
Only 0.1% of world are heavy AI users and capacity is already
maxed. Advice: buy compute now — prices will only increase.
