# LeCun Said LLMs Are a Dead End, Then Revealed Meta Fudged Their Benchmarks — Summary

Five significant stories from the week, out of 200+ tracked:

**1. Healthcare race:** OpenAI launched ChatGPT Health (consumer) and
OpenAI for Healthcare (enterprise, HIPAA-compliant). Anthropic followed
with Claude for Healthcare (CMS/insurance connectors). Prior
authorization alone is a $30B annual administrative burden. Deeper story:
both companies positioning for IPOs — healthcare provides a compelling
public market narrative. Every healthcare AI startup's build-vs-buy
calculation just got rewritten.

**2. LeCun's departure from Meta:** Confirmed Meta fudged Llama 4
benchmarks using different model variants for different tests.
Zuckerberg sidelined the entire GenAI organization. LeCun reiterated
LLMs are a dead end and will found a startup pursuing alternative path
to intelligence. Fundamental disagreement with Amodei and others on
whether LLM scaling has a ceiling. 1–2 years to see who is right.

**3. Physical AI convergence:** Nvidia Ruben platform, Google DeepMind +
Boston Dynamics putting Gemini into Atlas robots, Nvidia Alpa NIO open
models for autonomous vehicles. Three technologies converging: multimodal
foundation models, good simulation environments, powerful edge inference
chips (Jetson T4000 at 4x compute). Nvidia building full stack to win
regardless of which robot company succeeds.

**4. Training data exhausted:** Wired report — OpenAI and Handshake AI
asking contractors to upload real work products from past employers.
Public internet and books are scraped. Next frontier: actual work
products (internal docs, project files, spreadsheets). Company
implication: internal data is becoming strategically valuable.

**5. Claude Code explosion:** Boris Cherny (creator) runs 5–10 parallel
Claude instances with accumulated rules in claude.md. Cursor built a
browser with ChatGPT-5.2 in one week — 3M lines of Rust, fourth
rendering engine in the world. Claude Co-work launched: Claude Code for
non-technical knowledge work. The industry is testing whether capability
translates to value in 2026.
