# The $125 Billion Secret: Amazon Told Wall Street One Thing and Employees Another — Summary

Amazon cut 30,000 corporate jobs — not because of culture problems,
but because they need the money for GPUs. Quarterly free cash flow
went negative (-$4.8B) as capex hit $125B, with 75% going to AI
infrastructure.

**The contradiction:** Revenue $180B (+13% YoY), AWS growing 20%,
net income up 38%, stock up 10% — then the largest layoff in
Amazon's 30-year history. 14,000 in October, 16,000 the next week.

**The math:** 30,000 employees x ~$200K all-in comp = $6B annual
savings. Against -$4.8B quarterly FCF, that $6B is critical. It
equals the cost of Project Rainier (500K Trainium 2 chips). Amazon
also raised $12B in debt for data centers.

**Capex trajectory:** $83B (2024) to $125B (2025), with CFO guiding
higher for 2026. Amazon adding 3.8 GW of data center capacity
annually. On track to double computing power by 2027.

**Jassy's three-audience narrative:** Culture framing for employees
(too many layers), proactive optimization for investors (not financial
pressure), routine restructuring for regulators (not AI-driven). His
own June 2025 memo warned AI means "fewer people" — contradicting
the October earnings call.

**The real driver:** The culture problem is genuine (corporate
headcount tripled 2017-2022, decision-making slowed) but does not
explain the timing. FCF going negative and $125B capex guidance
announced the same week as layoffs — that explains the timing.

**Industry-wide pattern:** Goldman Sachs projects Big Five will spend
$1.15T on infrastructure 2025-2027. Aggregate capex consumes 94% of
operating cash flows (Bank of America). 1.1M job cuts across the
economy in 2025 (Challenger data). The companies cutting are not
struggling — they are among the most profitable in history.

**Key insight:** Workers are not being replaced by AI doing their
jobs. They are being replaced by the need to buy GPUs. Human capital
is being traded for compute capital. The shift is structural, not
cyclical. Every major tech company is running the same arithmetic.
