# Shopify's AI Memo Changed Hiring Forever — Summary

Tobi Lutke's April 2025 memo mandated: reflexive AI usage as baseline,
AI dominating prototype phase, AI in performance reviews, prove AI
can't do work before requesting headcount. Not new philosophy — the
Red Queen framework (improve 20–40%/year just to keep your role)
applied to AI.

Shopify's infrastructure preceded the memo by years. Farhan Tawer
brought GitHub Copilot pre-alpha in 2021 (year before ChatGPT). By
early 2023: 80% adoption. Built internal LLM proxy, dozens of MCP
servers (Slack, Salesforce, G Suite), open-sourced Roast code review
framework. Everyone gets access to everything — no spending quotas.

Fastest-growing AI user groups: not engineering — support and revenue
teams. Sales engineer uses Cursor as homepage with MCP-connected tools.
Revenue team deployed RFP agent learning from winning responses.

Headcount story: ~11,600 (end 2022, peak ZIRP) to ~8,100 (Dec 2024)
— primarily interest rate normalization, not AI. Then expanded
internship program 10x from 75 to 1,000+ interns seeking "AI centaurs."
U-shaped talent market: very senior leveraged people + AI-native juniors.

Copycat wave: Duolingo disaster (subscription cancellations, CEO walked
it back); Box more promising (teams keep AI savings for strategic
projects). Key lesson: Shopify's memo worked because of years of
infrastructure and culture.

Jensen Huang (Nov 2025): "every possible task" should be AI automated.
Called resistance "insane." Nvidia grew 29K to 36K employees, still
10K short.

Job market data: AI skill postings doubled 5% to 9% (2024–2025).
AI-fluent workers: 1M (2023) to 7M (2025). 66% enterprises reducing
entry-level hiring. 84% report significant skill gaps. AI/ML roles
take 89 days to fill. 90% use AI; only 9% self-grade as AI mature.

2026 trajectory: AI fluency becomes baseline like email, role boundaries
dissolve (designers submitting PRs), new coordination roles emerge,
compensation polarizes, entry-level squeeze intensifies, training gap
becomes strategic liability. Companies without AI infrastructure face
chicken-and-egg problem.

Bottom line: companies face extinction-level risk from AI inaction.
Tailwind (CSS framework) struggling despite more usage — business model
not competitive. Stagnation is slow-motion failure.
