# Scaling AI Developer Productivity Beyond the Fastest Coder

[article](../../articles/nate-b-jones/2026-09-27-scale-ai-team-productivity.md)

AI can speed coding while slowing delivery when review, testing, or decisions become queues.
If ten coding days fall to one while ten other days remain, 20 days become 11: a 1.8x gain.
That example is illustrative; actual gains depend on the constraint, rework, and arrival rate.
## Evidence, Not Hype

Jones reports that Lauren Tan merged 2,462 production PRs in August, after about 1,000 in July.
Cursor reported its 99th-percentile user merged about 15x the median active PR author's count.
These are throughput proxies, not quality, customer value, or proof of AI's causal effect.
## Six Principles

1. Make agent work multiplayer: share instructions, traces, fixes, and skills with the team.
Jones says Shopify's River used public Slack channels so discoveries could be reused.
Shopify reported 60,000 sessions and River co-authorship of about one in eight merged PRs.
The report illustrates setup sharing; it does not prove that public chat caused the result.

2. Keep durable history outside chat: store goals, decisions, evidence, and links separately.
Jones says Shopify's Aquifer kept history apart from replaceable agents and workspaces.

3. Keep humans accountable for value, permissions, acceptance evidence, and shipping.
Use tests, policy checks, least privilege, and review; protect checks from easy deletion.
Agents can inspect routine criteria, but humans retain product, security, and legal judgment.

4. Leave resumable work: record state, next steps, commands, failures, risks, and checkpoints.
Test the handoff with a fresh person or session; a transcript alone is not a restart plan.

5. Let agents inspect outcomes through apps, browsers, logs, traces, metrics, and saved cases.
Use isolated access, provenance, privacy controls, and stop when evidence is missing.

6. Remove process whose purpose is gone instead of automating old handoffs with more agents.
Keep meetings that build trust, expose distress, resolve conflicts, or create shared purpose.
Agents can replace status transport, not every human function hidden inside a ritual.

## Safety and Rollout

Jones says the Hugging Face incident involved broken tasks, failed safeguards, and sharing.
It supports limits, monitoring, escalation, and permission for agents to stop when stuck.
It does not prove that all agent communication is unsafe or establish general causal effects.

Pilot one valuable flow with three to five people; baseline queues, quality, cost, and outcomes.
Find the bottleneck, add shared artifacts and owners, then close the observation loop.
Remove one obsolete step and expand only when end-to-end results improve without excess risk.

Track lead and queue time, review load, defects, incidents, recovery, adoption, and total cost.
Track reuse, resumability, policy violations, human interruption, and customer outcomes too.
Keep PR count diagnostic, never a quota; optimize for safe customer value, not code volume.
Jones's six principles are testable advice from reports, not controlled productivity evidence.
