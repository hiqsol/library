# This Is Why You Are Still Slow Even With AI — Summary

Anthropic shipped Co-work in 10 days with 4 people using Claude Code,
releasing 60–100 times daily. Meanwhile most companies are writing
30-day implementation roadmaps. Execution capacity is no longer scarce
but all our work habits still protect it.

Manufacturing principle: eliminating a bottleneck relocates it. The
bottleneck has moved from execution to four areas:

- **Clarity** — knowing what is worth building. PRDs were hedges against
  expensive rework; now a PRD can cost more than shipping the product.
- **Ambition** — with 50 swings/year instead of 3–4, the risk is
  timidity, not building the wrong thing.
- **Distribution** — when everyone can build, getting into hands is the
  moat. Cognition partnered with Infosys (300K+ people) rather than
  building own distribution.
- **Relationships** — you cannot vibe-code a relationship. Durable
  advantage for individuals and companies alike.

Eight habits to break, all risk-management rituals from when execution
was expensive:

1. **Permission loop** — asking takes longer than doing
2. **Polish as procrastination** — 80% time on last 20% quality;
   rough version that exists beats polished version that doesn't
3. **Meetings as default** — 6 people x 1 hour = enough time to build
   the thing; replace meetings with demos
4. **Structured waiting** — waiting an hour now costs a prototype
5. **Planning before doing** — cut planning by 90%; plans don't survive
   reality contact
6. **Deck instead of demo** — build the working prototype instead
7. **Consensus before action** — cost has 100x-ed; let results create
   alignment
8. **Hoarding until ready** — ship early, find out you're wrong in a
   week not a month

Cursor: $1M to $500M ARR faster than any SaaS company in history.
Coinbase: single engineers refactoring codebases in days.

The chaos people feel is the gap between where the bottleneck has moved
and the habits they still have. Close that gap and the chaos makes
sense. The truly precious resources are now clarity, ambition,
distribution, and relationships.
