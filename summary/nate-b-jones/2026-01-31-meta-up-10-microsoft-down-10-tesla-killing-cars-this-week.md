# Meta Up 10%, Microsoft Down 10%, Tesla Killing Cars — Summary

[article](../../articles/nate-b-jones/2026-01-31-meta-up-10-microsoft-down-10-tesla-killing-cars-this-week.md)

Meta and Microsoft both beat earnings expectations and announced
massive AI spending — but Meta jumped 10% while Microsoft dropped
11%. The divergence: Meta controls its AI (ad engine, recommendation
algorithms) while 45% of Microsoft's $625B commercial backlog depends
on OpenAI, a company it does not control. Deutsche Bank called it a
"make-or-break moment."

Meta posted $8.88 EPS (vs. $8.16 expected), $59.9B revenue, and
guided $115-135B capex (up from $72B). Reality Labs still burns $6B
per quarter on the metaverse.

Apple chose Google/Gemini over Anthropic/Claude for Siri — reportedly
because Anthropic was too aggressive on pricing. Google is the only
hyperscaler where AI is an affordable bet relative to search revenue.

A Waymo robo-taxi struck a child in Santa Monica. NHTSA and NTSB both
opened investigations. Waymo vehicles also passed stopped school
buses 19 times in Austin. Waymo's defensive statistical response was
tone-deaf. The core issue is accountability, not statistics.

Nvidia invested $2B in CoreWeave targeting 5 GW of data center
capacity by 2030. Microsoft signed a $750M deal with Perplexity.
Both signal a shift from first-wave AI spending (model development)
to second-wave (deployment infrastructure).

Tesla is discontinuing Model S and Model X to convert Fremont factory
lines to Optimus robot production — targeting 1M robots/year starting
late 2026. Also: $2B investment in xAI, 1M+ FSD subscriptions,
robo-taxi expanding to 7 metro areas in H1, $20B total capex plan.

Anthropic closed funding at $350B valuation (nearly 2x September),
led by GIC and Coatue, with potential $10-15B more from Microsoft
and Nvidia.

OpenClaw (formerly Clawbot) crossed 100,000 GitHub stars — an AI
agent connecting to messaging platforms for autonomous task execution.
Growth is hyper-exponential with significant security implications.

Through-line: the phase of AI potential-talk is over. Every major
player has made irreversible, expensive commitments. Execution against
those bets is all that remains.
