# $1,000 a Day in AI Costs — Summary

The fundamental unit of computing is shifting from instructions to
tokens — units of purchased intelligence. This is not a tools upgrade;
it is a paradigm change.

Key spending data points: StrongDM's 3-person team targets $1,000/day
in token spend with no handwritten code. Cursor's AWS costs spiked
$6M to $12M in one month. Anthropic spent $2.66B on AWS through
September 2025 against ~$2.55B in revenue. Perplexity spent 164% of
its 2024 revenue on compute providers.

Per-token costs are falling 10-200x/year (GPT-4 equivalent: $20/M
tokens in 2022, ~$0.40 today). Jevons' Paradox applies: cheaper
intelligence drives massively more consumption. Average enterprise
AI spend: $85K/month, up 36% YoY. A16Z survey: enterprise LLM spend
hit $7M in 2025, projected $11M+ in 2026.

OpenAI reportedly plans agent tiers: $2K/month (knowledge workers),
$10K (specialized coding), $20K (AI research).

Three developer career tracks are emerging:
1. **Orchestrators** — specify outcomes, manage intelligence budgets
2. **Systems Builders** — build agent infrastructure, routing layers
3. **Domain Translators** — combine AI fluency with deep domain
   expertise (largest track, mostly unrecognized)

Most exposed: mid-range developers writing competent application code
without deep systems or domain expertise.

AI-native companies run 3-5x revenue per employee vs. traditional
SaaS ($10M ARR startup: ~15 people vs. 55-70 at traditional SaaS).
Minimum viable team size is approaching one. The market is splitting:
enterprises compete on token volume and horizontal scale; small
builders compete on distribution, trust, and domain specificity.

The startup playbook: a $200/month Claude Max subscription aimed
precisely at a known market creates more value than a $20K/month
enterprise agent budget pointed at the wrong problem.
