~$100/month) shipped with Opus 4.6 in February 2026. A Goldman
analyst confirmed the output — a full operating model plus board

and color schemes — produces slides matching your design system. Major

custom templates.

Financial data connectors: Anthropic partnered with Moody's, London

data feeds. Pre-built financial skills for DCF models, comparable
company analysis, due diligence packs.

Enterprise validation: Goldman Sachs deploying Claude in accounting/
compliance (production, months ongoing). AIG: document reviews 5x
faster, accuracy 75% to 90%+. Norway's sovereign wealth fund ($1.7T):
estimated 213,000 hours saved.

Workflows demonstrated: 3-year operating model, board decks, Series B
pitch decks, due diligence data packs, comparable analysis, strategy
scoring, sales enablement, HR analytics (2K survey responses), program
management status decks, plus daily grind (VLOOKUP debugging, Power
Query, conditional formatting).

The "context layer" — AI's understanding of data, brand, audience,
goals — sits between the application layer (Microsoft) and the data
layer (databases). When one intelligence spans both Excel and
PowerPoint, translation cost drops toward zero. Context layer
compounds with every model upgrade automatically.

Microsoft is becoming a dumb pipe: added Claude to Copilot in
September 2025. Same pattern as AT&T becoming a pipe for Google/
Netflix. Intelligence layer compounds; application layer is frozen.

The upgrade cycle is unprecedented: Opus 4.5 to 4.6 happened
overnight, nobody installed anything. Every Claude-powered tool gets
smarter with each model release.

The slop problem: AI makes it as easy to produce polished garbage as
excellent work. Estimated cost: $186/employee/month in wasted time.
Taste — distinguishing purposeful output from hollow output — becomes
a critical skill.

Core thesis: execution premium (building models, writing code) is
evaporating. What remains valuable: framing the right question,
knowing which analysis matters, human judgment. Organizations with
judgment will massively outexecute those with the same tools but
without it.
