# Going Slower Feels Safer, But Your Domain Expertise Won't Save You — Summary

[article](../../articles/nate-b-jones/2026-02-09-going-slower-feels-safer-but-your-domain-expertise-wont-save.md)

Two collapses are happening simultaneously in knowledge work:

**Horizontal collapse:** Engineer, PM, marketer, analyst, designer, ops
are converging into one meta-competency — orchestrating AI agents. Gartner
predicts ~50% of enterprise apps will integrate task-specific AI agents by
end of 2026, up from <5% in 2025 (8x increase). 57% of companies claim
AI agents in production as of 2025. Domain expertise becomes foundational
but not differentiating without AI orchestration skills.

Key concept: "software-shaped intent." Even non-technical workers need to
think about what agents can deliver within their technical ecosystem —
tool sets, memory, workflows — and frame requests as software-shaped
problems (reading/writing data, presenting it usefully).

**Temporal collapse:** Career leverage is compressing into the present.
SWE-bench went from 4% solved (2023) to ~90-95% solved (2025). The
doubling time itself is shrinking. Traditional career planning assumed
time to learn, apply, compound. That assumption is now catastrophically
wrong. Skills that matter in 2027 are being defined by people engaging
now. No mature state to wait for.

Bike analogy: going slower on AI feels safer but makes balance harder.
Going faster lets patterns solidify and feels more stable. Expertise now
depreciates unless continuously updated, and the depreciation rate is
accelerating.

Follow the money: Big tech AI capex ~$500B in 2025, $500B+ in 2026.
Big Five plan $2T+ in AI assets over four years. The money is committed.
There is no career path that does not include AI.

Resistance options: if you genuinely want to exit tech, that is valid.
But staying in AI-adjacent industries while resisting AI will not work.
Trying ChatGPT once in 2022, finding hallucinations, and walking away is
no longer viable.

Core message: lean in with curiosity. Going faster with AI is safer than
going slower. Each engagement builds unconscious pattern recognition
that compounds over time.
