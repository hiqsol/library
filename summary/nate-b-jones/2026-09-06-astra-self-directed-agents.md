# GPT-6 Astra Doesn't Need Your Instructions Anymore.

[article](../../articles/nate-b-jones/2026-09-06-astra-self-directed-agents.md)

 Jones calls Astra a practical AGI threshold because it can choose methods and sustain broad
jobs. That is his interpretation, not a settled definition or proof of consciousness.

## Examples and capabilities

- Ethan Mollick reportedly gave it tens of thousands of emails plus writing, contacts, and a
  calendar.
- Over five days, it chose tools and built a personal knowledge system used twice daily.
- Another reported run checked 41 financial documents and found every planted error.
- Playco used Unity and Godot integration to build and test all 10 ideas before selecting one.
- Vercel's Ship Closer checks agreement between launch records and the public changelog.

## What persistent agents change

- Delegation expands from bounded prompts to standing responsibilities without a natural end.
- Work in software with inspectable evidence is especially suitable for iterative agent
  execution.
- Agents from different providers may coordinate inside the same business systems.
- Memory accumulated over six months could make an agent much more useful than a fresh instance.
- That history also creates dependence resembling an employee's accumulated tribal knowledge.

## The remaining human responsibilities

- Jones frames the next barrier as trust, using a 98–99% versus fully dependable comparison.
- Those percentages are illustrative, not measured reliability guarantees.
- Authority must distinguish routine spending from a major commitment such as a $50,000 car.
- Managers still choose priorities, resolve conflicts, and own the consequences.
- Junior workers may lose traditional practice opportunities; managing agents is one possible
  route.

Before assigning an ongoing job, define what the agent may read, remember, initiate, and
promise. Identify who monitors and corrects it. Jones expects these operational decisions to
matter before society reaches agreement on the term AGI.
