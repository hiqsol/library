# Why the Smartest AI Bet Right Now Has Nothing to Do With AI — Summary

The Davos "abundance for all" narrative from Musk, Amodei, and others
is the wrong frame. The practical frame is the **bottleneck economy**:
value concentrates wherever scarcity migrates to.

Cognizant research claims AI could unlock $4.5 trillion in US labor
productivity — but only if businesses implement it effectively. Most
have not done the hard work.

**Four major AI-era bottlenecks:**

1. **Physical infrastructure (atoms, not bits):** Hyperscale data
   centers consume 100+ MW. Training frontier models requires weeks of
   sustained exaFLOPS. Google is bottlenecked on grid connections. DRAM
   prices are skyrocketing. Permitting takes years. Trade craft
   salaries have nearly doubled per Jensen Huang.

2. **Trust deficit:** AI collapses generation costs but trust gets
   harder, not cheaper. Synthetic vs. authentic becomes
   indistinguishable. Trust reduces transaction costs; degraded trust
   raises them. Value goes to whoever can verify, authenticate, and
   certify — "trust banks" for the 21st century.

3. **Integration gap:** AI has general capacity but no specific
   context. It does not know your codebase, your board, your
   competitive dynamics. Tacit organizational knowledge is not
   promptable. The $4.5T value gap lives here.

4. **Coordination problem:** AI does not make humans agree or align.
   It may make coordination harder. IMF says 40% of global jobs
   affected; "we don't know how to make it inclusive."

**Individual bottlenecks (the fractal principle):**

- **Taste and judgment** — when generation is cheap, curation is
  expensive. People thriving are narrowing focus earlier.
- **Problem finding over problem solving** — specifying the right
  problem remains human. Direction-setting eclipses execution skill.
- **Context as a moat** — institutional knowledge becomes an
  individual moat, like data is for companies. Juniors lose the grunt
  work that historically built this context.
- **Execution and follow-through** — AI generates plans but humans
  must decide, commit, persist, and navigate politics.
- **Tolerance for ambiguity** — ability to metabolize change without
  freezing while maintaining long-term execution.

**Key takeaway:** Intelligence is getting cheaper. Abundance shifts
where scarcity lives. The only question that matters: where are the
bottlenecks, and are you positioning yourself to solve them?
