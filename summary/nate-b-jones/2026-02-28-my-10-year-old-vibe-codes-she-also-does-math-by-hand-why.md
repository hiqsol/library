# My 10-Year-Old Vibe Codes and Does Math by Hand — Summary

*Nature* published a peer-reviewed argument that AGI has arrived. 86% of
students globally use AI in learning. Someone built an entire medical school
curriculum with Claude Code in two weeks (450 lectures, 16,000 figures,
100M tokens, 99% flawless). Two billion kids are in schools designed for a
20th century economy.

**The calculator parallel:** 1970s schools banned calculators. The transition
worked because students learned mechanics first, then the tool extended their
foundation. We are in that moment again — but for every cognitive task.

**Foundation matters because specification matters.** The most important
finding from autonomous agents: output quality = human specification quality.
Same agent architecture negotiated $4,200 off a car in one case, sent 500
unsolicited messages in another. The variable was the human's ability to
specify. You cannot write a good spec for something you don't understand.

**Physical skills build cognitive infrastructure:**
- Reading books: struggle with text is the learning
- Math by hand: intuitive feel for magnitude and proportion
- Writing by hand: memory and comprehension benefits

**Vibe coding teaches specification.** Author's 10-year-old: "add enemies"
produced broken behavior. After articulating what she actually wanted
(spawn location, speed, behavior), she got working results. Kids doing
this are specifying requirements, decomposing tasks, debugging their intent.

**Cognitive offloading danger:** 75% of teenagers use AI chatbots for
emotional support. College professors report students who can no longer read
full chapters or synthesize arguments. Writing quality has collapsed.
Karpathy: "You will never be able to detect AI in homework. Full stop."
Detection tools are mathematically impossible to implement correctly.

**Metacognition is the bridge skill.** Not what you know or what the machine
knows, but your capacity to move between the two strategically.

**Seven principles for parents:**
1. Foundation before leverage
2. Specification is the new literacy
3. Be a director, not a passenger
4. Sequence the autonomy (readiness model, not age-gated)
5. Teach kids to catch the machine
6. Build, don't browse (creation over consumption)
7. Attempt before augmenting

**The trap** is not that AI will be too powerful. It is that AI will be so
seamlessly helpful that children never develop the tolerance for difficulty
that real learning requires.
