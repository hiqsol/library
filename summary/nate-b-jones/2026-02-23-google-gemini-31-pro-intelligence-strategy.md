# Google's Gemini 3.1 Pro: Intelligence Strategy — Summary

Nate B Jones analyzes why Google shipped the top-benchmarked AI model at
a fraction of competitors' prices and doesn't need you to use it.

## Key Facts

- Gemini 3.1 Pro leads on 13 of 16 benchmarks.
- ARC AGI2: 77.1% (up from 31.1% on Gemini 3 Pro, 90 days prior) —
  largest single-generation reasoning gain ever for a frontier model.
- Opus 4.6: 68.8% on same benchmark. GPT 5.2: slightly lower.
- Pricing: $2/M input, $12/M output — ~7.5x cheaper than Opus 4.6.
- Google's annual free cash flow: $100B+. CapEx: $93B (mostly AI).
- Ironwood TPU (7th gen): 10x compute, ~50% energy cost, 9,216 chips/pod.
- Anthropic signed for 1M TPUs (tens of billions). Meta negotiating similar.
- 650M monthly active Gemini users (+44% quarterly).

## Core Thesis

Google is not playing the product race — it is solving intelligence.
Demis Hassabis's 15-year mission: solve intelligence, then solve everything
else. Google's $100B cash engine funds this without needing model revenue.

## Three Models, Three Strategies

- **Gemini 3.1 Pro:** strongest pure (naked) reasoner.
- **Opus 4.6:** strongest equipped reasoner — tools, sustained agentic work.
- **Codex 5.3:** strongest specialist coder.

When given tools, Opus catches up or leads (289 ELO gap on GDPVal).

## Seven Dimensions of Difficulty

The video's central framework — "hard" is not one thing:

1. **Reasoning** — deep logical deduction (Gemini excels)
2. **Effort** — large but straightforward tasks (agentic AI excels)
3. **Coordination** — aligning teams, routing work (Opus excels)
4. **Emotional intelligence** — no model handles reliably
5. **Judgment/willpower** — courage problems, not compute problems
6. **Domain expertise** — lived experience vs. training data
7. **Ambiguity** — defining the question, not computing the answer

Pure reasoning is typically ~10% of knowledge work. A model for pure
reasoning helps the demanding 10%; a model for tools and sustained work
helps the other 90%.

## Google's Vertical Stack (Unique Advantage)

Transistor design → TPU silicon → cloud infrastructure → model training →
distribution (Search, Android, YouTube, Chrome) → fundamental research
(DeepMind, Nobel Prize for AlphaFold). No competitor has this full stack.
Jeff Dean: AI is shrinking TPU design cycles from 2 years to 6–9 months.

## Practical Advice

1. **Map model routing to your domain** — route reasoning to Gemini,
   coding to Claude Code, quick research to Flash, deep analysis to Opus.
   Model routing is a compounding skill.
2. **Decompose your work by difficulty dimension** — each is automated on
   a different timeline. Reasoning and effort are yielding now; emotional
   intelligence and judgment are not.
3. **Build the skill of evaluating AI output** — as models improve, the
   ability to judge whether output is good becomes the most valuable and
   rarest skill.
