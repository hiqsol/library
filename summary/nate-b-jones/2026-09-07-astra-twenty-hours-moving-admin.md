# There Are Jobs You Could Never Give AI. I Gave GPT-6 Astra 20 Hours Of Admin.

[article](../../articles/nate-b-jones/2026-09-07-astra-twenty-hours-moving-admin.md)

 Jones tested Astra on a simulated household move involving more than 20 hours of
administration. His main claim is that agents can increasingly carry whole jobs across tools and
changing plans.

## What changes

- Earlier models already handle bounded tasks such as checking doctors or preparing
  appointments.
- Astra's reported advantage is sustained coordination across documents, websites, and
  calendars.
- Missing information should block only the affected tasks, not the entire assignment.
- Jones compares this shift to Claude Code's move from code fragments to complete assignments.

## The manager loop

- Give one manager agent the outcome, destination, deadline, budget, and relevant constraints.
- Let it interview you about housing, schools, doctors, vehicles, pets, and available records.
- The manager assigns execution work, tracks dependencies, checks progress, and reports back.
- Independent tasks can run together while dependent work waits for required decisions.
- Jones's move was simulated; the video does not establish a completed real-world relocation.

## Recipe cards and responsibility

- A recipe card describes the job, inputs, access, deliverables, escalation, and approval rules.
- It includes responses to missing documents, failed logins, conflicting sources, and blocked
  sites.
- Jones's paid companion offers 23 cards; the general method does not require a giant prompt.
- Agents prepare and check options; people retain consequential choices and responsibility.
- Jones favors Fable 5.1 for ambiguous exploration and Astra for sustained, context-heavy
  execution.

Before delegating, ask whether the whole job fits the agent, whether it needs agent supervision,
and which choices must return to you. The intended gain is less administrative coordination and
more capacity to make meaningful decisions.
