# Everyone You Know Is About to Try Claude — Summary

**Published:** 2026-03-04 15:00

After Anthropic refused the Pentagon and the White House retaliated, Claude
became the #1 app in America. Millions of new users are treating it as a
ChatGPT drop-in replacement. That approach will fail — the models are built
on fundamentally different training philosophies.

**ChatGPT** is trained via RLHF optimizing for user satisfaction. It tends
toward agreeable, expansive, warm responses. **Claude** is trained via
Constitutional AI against explicit principles (helpful, honest, avoid harm),
producing more concise, more critical, more context-sensitive responses.

Seven principles for using Claude effectively:

1. **Claude pushes back more.** Sycophancy is rooted in ChatGPT's training.
   Claude is more likely to flag concerns and question framing. The most
   expensive AI mistakes are plans that go unchallenged.

2. **Describe your situation, not desired output.** Claude does more with
   well-framed context. Spend sentences on what you're dealing with before
   stating what you want.

3. **Give Claude existing work to edit.** Claude excels at structural editing;
   ChatGPT polishes at sentence level. Blind tests: Claude won 4/8 rounds
   vs. ChatGPT's 1/8. Claude scored 85% structural coherence vs. 78%.

4. **Use extended thinking.** Claude shows its chain of reasoning (up to 54%
   improvement on hard tasks). You can see and redirect the reasoning mid-
   stream, unlike hidden inference compute models.

5. **Build workspace projects with operating rules.** Not "help me with
   marketing" but detailed role, audience, constraints, uploaded docs.
   Pixel Peak: Claude hit 94% instruction compliance vs. ChatGPT's 87%.

6. **Co-work: Claude works on your computer.** Desktop agent (macOS, Windows
   coming) that opens, reads, edits files and executes multi-step tasks
   autonomously with folder-level permissions.

7. **Know what you lose.** No image/video generation, weaker math/science,
   less web research breadth, no persistent memory advantage, no GPT
   marketplace. Use ChatGPT for those; use Claude for its strengths.

Key insight: tool differences shape how you think about AI over time. A tool
that pushes back trains you to invite challenge. Multi-tool fluency is a
breakthrough skill of 2026.
