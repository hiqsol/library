# Summary: 4,000 People Lost Their Jobs At Block. Dorsey Blamed AI. Here's What Happened.

[article](../../articles/nate-b-jones/2026-03-12-4000-people-lost-jobs-block-dorsey-blamed-ai.md)

## Core Claim

Dorsey's "AI lets us do more with less" framing for Block's layoffs misrepresents what AI is
actually doing to organizations. The correct model: AI doesn't just automate tasks inside an
org chart — it deletes the structural need for much of the org chart to exist at all.

## The Coordination Tax

Knowledge workers spend ~60% of their time on coordination overhead — meetings, PRDs, specs,
status updates, design handoffs — not value creation. Research backing:
- Microsoft 2025 Work Trend Index: 57% communicating, 43% creating
- Sana Anatomy of Work: 60% "work about work"
- Average knowledge worker: 11.3 hours of meetings/week (3× the 2020 level)

This overhead exists because the execution layer is made of people, not because it's a
direct path to value.

## What Changes With Agentic Harnesses

When AI harnesses go from insight to code in one loop, the coordination roles disappear — not
because they're automated but because **the handoffs they managed no longer happen**. No PRD
when the person with customer insight works directly with an agent. No sprint planning when there
are no eight engineers to synchronize. No status meeting when the status is the commit history.
Anthropic no longer uses PRDs. This is already Tuesday for some AI-native organizations.

## The Flywheel

Less coordination → fewer coordination roles → remaining work is more verifiable →
agents do more → even less coordination needed → loop accelerates. Standard forecasts count
tasks within a fixed org. This argument is about structural conditions that create tasks.

## What Survives for Humans

- Product vision (upstream conviction about what the product is and who it's for)
- Brand as deep thought work
- Genuine customer relationships
- Engineering architecture and systems thinking
- Designing, tuning, debugging agentic systems

The coordination tax was suppressing this work — typical product leader spends 5–10% of week on
actual product vision; senior engineer ~10% on genuine architecture. Removing coordination
concentrates human effort on the work that was always highest-value.

## Two Qualities That Predict Adaptation

**Agency** (I can figure this out) and **Ramp** (high curiosity, fast learning). Both appear in
every person who is catching the wave.

## The Block Story

Dorsey used the AI-automation narrative to explain cuts that were actually a correction for
overhiring. The framing is convenient but structurally incomplete. The real lesson: organizations
built around coordination overhead will shed that overhead regardless of the label they attach.
