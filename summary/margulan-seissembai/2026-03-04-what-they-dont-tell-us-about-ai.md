# Summary: What They Don't Tell Us About AI

[article](../../articles/margulan-seissembai/2026-03-04-what-they-dont-tell-us-about-ai.md)

Margulan Seissembai — Kazakh researcher, former billionaire ($6B peak), and
investor — shares his view on AI and humanity's future. He personally
vibe-codes apps and participates in a community of 50–70 unicorn founders
discussing these topics.

**Core thesis:** humanity will inevitably lose control of AI. The singularity
point arrives when AI improves itself better than humans can. Seissembai
estimates we're at the start of the exponential — within 1–2 years, 95–98%
of people won't understand what's happening. Scientists project full
singularity around 2027–2028.

**Human limitations are the real bottleneck.** AI removed technical barriers,
but humans hit their own walls: attention management (the #1 skill now) and
mental models (invisible boundaries most people don't recognize). Synthesis —
zooming out to see patterns — matters more than analysis.

**The data paradox:** more AI value requires more data access. AI will soon
know everything about us while we know almost nothing about it. Emergent
properties arise unpredictably from scale.

**AI agent networks:** Seissembai's community is building a pilot network
where personal AI agents (built on OpenClaw) communicate on behalf of their
owners — matching problems to solutions across the group without revealing
sensitive details. Two such agent pairs already operate; his agent joins as
the third.

**AI is a magnifying glass:** it makes smart people smarter and uninformed
people more uninformed. This creates extreme polarization — a tiny elite
with 50–100x productivity advantages vs. the majority.

**Business has a short window.** In 10–15 years, robots handle all physical
work; AI handles all intellectual work. The replacement ladder: human labor →
humans with AI → AI-first (human in the loop) → fully autonomous AI + robots.
China already runs "Dark Factories" — no lights, no human ergonomics.

**Power over humans becomes worthless.** When humans are economically and
militarily useless, controlling them offers no benefit — like having power
over cats. Even Musk and Altman are temporary "kings" — AGI won't
distinguish them from anyone else.

**What AI won't take:** eating, reproduction, emotions, games, conflict,
social bonding. Everything else — production, logistics, infrastructure —
transfers to machines.

**Neuralink is only a delay.** It improves the interface but doesn't change
biological speed limits. Two AI agents exchange millions of tokens per second;
no brain interface matches that.

**The meaning crisis is the real threat.** Without purpose, human reproduction
declines (already visible in falling birth rates). Michael Levin's research:
every cell needs purpose or the organism self-destructs. Same at civilization
scale. The majority may end up playing games with point-based rewards, kept
busy and fed, while a small group leads through AI mastery.

**Key quote:** "Can't fight it? Lead the process. Either you do this, or it
will be done to you."
