# Summary

Colonel Petro Chernyk analyzes the US military operation against Iran and its
consequences for Ukraine and global order.

- Iran's missile activity has fallen 90%, drone activity 95%; over 15,000
  targets struck by the US since the operation began.
- Iran had up to 2,000 heavy missiles and ~500 launchers; Chernyk estimates
  two-thirds destroyed, but mountainous terrain and tunnel complexes make
  full elimination difficult.
- Kharg Island was struck heavily (90+ military targets), but oil terminals
  were deliberately spared; Iran's navy is essentially destroyed.
- The US is not entering the Hormuz Strait directly, pushing responsibility
  to Europe and Asia because the US imports only 2% of its Gulf oil; China
  imports 90% through that strait.
- Six NATO/allied nations (UK, France, Germany, Italy, Netherlands, Japan)
  have agreed to join strait patrols.
- Chernyk warns of a "Stockholm syndrome" in Ukraine -- growing sympathy for
  Iran seeded by Russian information operations. He rejects any equivalence
  between the US-Iran operation and Russia's war on Ukraine.
- The Iranian regime directly participated in the genocide of Ukrainians via
  the Shahed-136 drone. He distinguishes between the regime and the Iranian
  people but notes ordinary workers built and delivered the weapons.
- He invokes the Nuremberg precedent: the entire German people underwent
  denazification, not just party members. He argues a similar accountability
  standard applies to Iran.
- China has provided Iran no significant military aid despite their ties; this
  mirrors the dynamic where China armed Afghan mujahideen covertly against the
  USSR while now calculating the risks of escalation with the US.
- Russian oligarchs and sanctions-evasion networks based in Dubai are fleeing
  amid the conflict, collapsing a key supply chain for Western microelectronics
  reaching Russia's war economy.
- Chernyk expects the operation to conclude by summer; oil prices will not
  reach the $150/barrel threshold needed to cause a systemic shock.
- On NATO Article 5: Hungary and Slovakia would likely block activation even
  if Russia attacked the Baltic states. Russia could use a mass infantry
  assault with small arms (millions of Kalashnikovs remain in storage) to
  trigger a crisis without needing advanced weapons.
- Chernyk sees the US as re-asserting global hegemony; argues an American
  hegemon is preferable to a Chinese or Russian one.
- Closing message to Ukrainians: the coming decades will bring continuous
  warfare. Follow Bandera's principle -- rely on your own strength, use cold
  reason in war, not emotion.
