Vitaliy Portnikov on Feigin Live (March 24, 2026) analyzes how Trump's Iran
war is going sideways and what it means for Ukraine.

## Iran: unexpected escalation

US/Israel planned a simple war — destroy Iran's missiles and nukes, regime
falls or capitulates. Instead:
- Iran struck Gulf states (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Qatar, Oman) — total
  surprise to Washington and Jerusalem
- Hormuz Strait blockade threatens global energy and food crisis; fertilizer
  shipments disrupted before planting season
- Hezbollah reactivated despite Nasrallah's killing
- Iran revealed 4,000km range missiles and maneuverable warheads that
  US/Israeli intelligence didn't know about
- Decentralized command structure means eliminating leaders doesn't stop
  operations

## Antimissile crisis

The world is running out of interceptors. US burned through expensive
Patriot missiles; new production starts 2027. If war continues, Ukraine,
Israel, and Saudi Arabia will have nothing to shoot down incoming missiles.
Ironically, Ukraine could help the US conserve ammunition — but Trump
rejects Ukrainian cooperation.

## Trump's pattern

Same playbook for Iran and Ukraine: threats, ultimatums, claims of
imminent victory, then zigzag to "peace talks." Told press that Zelensky
and Putin are "close to a deal." Defense Secretary Hegseth says "we
negotiate with bombs" — Trump jokes he's "the one who doesn't want this
resolved."

## 2026-2027 outlook

Portnikov warns of cascading crises: gas station closures in Bangladesh
are "just the beginning" — food shortages, regime changes, radical
Islamist movements across the Global South. Iran wants to lure the US
into a ground war producing American casualties. Trump faces a choice:
ground operations or a deal that leaves the regime intact.
