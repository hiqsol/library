# Summary: How to Accumulate $30,000 Capital

Ukrainian investor in Poland grew capital from $20,000 to $31,500
in 6 months. Three core principles:

## 1. Capital Is Built Outside the Market

- With small accounts (<$10K), market returns are irrelevant
- 80-90% of his growth = regular deposits, not investment gains
- Monthly deposits: $1,000+
- Use diversified ETFs, skip stock picking until $200K+ capital
- Individual stocks with small capital = wasted time and extra
  taxes/commissions

## 2. Every Dollar Must Work

- Bank cash → savings accounts (even at low rates)
- Stablecoins → staking
- Medium-term → government bonds (~4% on US Treasuries)
- Brokerage → nearly fully invested
- Only ~$1,000 uninvested as emergency buffer
- Even waiting for a dip? Park cash in bonds, not idle accounts

## 3. Increase Your Hourly Value

- Factory worker leveraging weekend premiums (2x Sat, 3x Sun)
- Worked 3/4 Sundays in peak months
- Consulting income: 1 hour = 8 hours of factory work
- Side income: courses, Telegram channel, YouTube
- Focus energy where hourly value is highest

## Bottom Line

At the start, focus on income growth and deposits, not which ETF
to buy. Stock selection matters only when capital exceeds $100K+.
Every financial trick compounds: savings accounts, weekend shifts,
side gigs, staking — all add up on a long time horizon.
