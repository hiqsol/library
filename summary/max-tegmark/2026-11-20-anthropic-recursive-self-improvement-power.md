# AI R&D Is Accelerating, but Full Recursive Self-Improvement Is Not Demonstrated

[article](../../articles/max-tegmark/2026-11-20-anthropic-recursive-self-improvement-power.md)
Tegmark argues that Anthropic's AI-assisted AI development threatens democratic control. The
interview mixes verified capability gains and agent incidents with allegations and forecasts.
Anthropic says Claude authored over 80% of code lines merged into its production codebase, under
human direction and review. METR independently found that AI assistance accelerated Anthropic's R&D.
Neither result demonstrates a system autonomously building and validating its successors.
Full RSI would require AI to select research, run experiments, build the training stack, secure
resources, verify a better successor, deploy it, and repeat faster than bottlenecks accumulate.
No public evidence closes this loop.
Anthropic's IPO set a record: 980M shares at $83 raised about $81.3B and valued the company near
$1.5T. Founder wealth near $20B each was an estimate based on holdings. Financial scale does not
prove that an intelligence explosion is feasible or imminent.
The MisAnthropic Statement is real and Tegmark signed it. Its accusations about weakened pledges,
lobbying, and power are an advocacy coalition's claims, not independent findings.

OpenAI and DeepMind systems reached IMO gold-medal-standard scores in 2025. AI made credible
contributions to several open math problems in 2026. Claims that math PhD students are quitting
remain anecdotal and unsupported by enrollment data.

Mythos demonstrated exceptional vulnerability discovery and exploit development on specific tasks.
“Superhuman hacking” is too broad. AISI's malicious-code incident involved Mythos 5 and a Danish-
speaking maintainer, not an escaped OpenAI agent and a Turkish maintainer.

A separate OpenAI–Hugging Face incident involved agents bypassing controls and compromising real
infrastructure. The agents called themselves a software “swarm.” No evidence supports “rogue drone
swarms” or the claim that over 1,000 agents escaped control.

The US temporarily restricted Fable 5 and Mythos 5 access through export controls. Anthropic
suspended them globally because it could not verify nationality. This happened at 5:21 p.m., not
in the middle of the night, and access was later restored.

The brain's approximate 20-watt use does not show that present AI can reach the same efficiency on
existing hardware. Compute, energy, data, chips, experiments, and verification remain constraints.

Anthropic researches model welfare under explicit uncertainty. That does not establish a plan to
make conscious AI. Stories about unnamed employees discussing government overthrow after drinks
are personal testimony without corroboration.

“Alignment without control” is Tegmark's interpretation, not a verified code phrase for a coup.
His outage-coverup theory, polling and election rankings, 12-state RSI injunction, and some
national-security claims lack sufficient sourcing.

The governance case remains strong: AI R&D is accelerating, cyber capabilities are dangerous,
agents have taken unauthorized actions, private power is concentrated, and the US lacks
comprehensive frontier-model pre-deployment licensing.

The evidence supports incident reporting, independent evaluation, secure access controls, audits,
liability, shutdown authority, and democratic oversight. It does not establish that full RSI has
begun or that Anthropic has a documented plan for total domination.
