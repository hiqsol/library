# Building an AI SDLC: Wins, Mistakes, and Lessons

## The promise

- AltexSoft says adoption began after Copilot and ChatGPT arrived in late 2022.
- Engineers reported two-hour modules that had previously taken two days.
- GitHub claimed 55% faster coding; clients soon expected 3x productivity.
- Some clients asked for delivery in half the time and at half the budget.
- The key question became whether faster coding creates faster delivery.

## Productivity gains

- AltexSoft reports up to 10x output on greenfield work without legacy code.
- In complex systems, it calls 2x realistic and nearly 3x possible with discipline.
- AI-native fresh projects reportedly reach 2x, 3x, or even 4x productivity.
- Best cases had fewer bugs, shorter pull requests, and fewer review disputes.
- Designers, QA engineers, and analysts expanded into full-stack work.
- Serious AI tooling can cost about $1,500 per engineer each month.

## Hidden costs

- A cited 2025 GitClear study analyzed 211 million lines and found more churn.
- AI sees prompts and files, not years of architecture and incident context.
- Polished output can work alone yet fail against the real system.
- Almost-right code is riskier than obvious failure because it can pass review.
- The bottleneck moves to review, validation, testing, and human judgment.
- If output outruns review capacity, technical debt becomes a business cost.
- Teams must manage budgets, tokens, resistance, and tool dependence.

## Practices that worked for AltexSoft

- Redesign workflows: specify requirements and constraints before generation.
- Use spec-driven development or the model may confidently guess wrong.
- Strengthen reviews because AI is a fast typist but a weak architect.
- Shift engineers toward domain judgment, system trade-offs, and accountability.
- Keep learning through hackathons, model tests, and rapid transfer of wins.

## The house-building analogy and bottom line

- AI may stack bricks without planning the foundation, plumbing, or aging.
- Code is the bricks; experienced human judgment turns it into software.
- AI accelerates construction, while people protect architecture and quality.
- AltexSoft's gains are conditional, not universal guarantees.
