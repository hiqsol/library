# Summary

Terence Tao (Fields Medal, UCLA) interviewed by Dwarkesh Patel on AI's role in mathematics.

## Core thesis: breadth vs. depth
- AI excels at breadth; humans at depth. Science must be redesigned to exploit AI breadth.
- New paradigm: AI maps entire fields, finds easy results, identifies hard islands; humans work
  the islands.

## Erdős problems benchmark
- ~50 of 1,100 open Erdős problems solved with AI assistance, then plateau hit.
- AI good at one-shot solving; bad at partial progress or identifying intermediate steps.
- Three attempts to have frontier models attack all problems simultaneously: no new solutions.
- Mountain analogy: AI jumps 2m and finds all short walls; tall walls remain untouched.

## The bullish waterline argument
- When AI reaches Terence Tao level, it can apply that intelligence to a *million* problems
  simultaneously — qualitatively more powerful than human experts at the same level.
- Even human-average AI breadth is unprecedented. The same ceiling that limits AI now is the
  reason to be maximally bullish once it rises.

## Lean and formal proofs
- Lean verifies every proof step — enables ablation, modular analysis, AI post-processing.
- Incomprehensible proofs are not a problem: the artifact can be mined for insight.
- Multi-AI workflows emerging: one AI generates strategy, another critiques, another does lit
  survey; some Erdős solutions came from ongoing human + AI collaborative chains.

## The missing tool: semiformal strategy languages
- Lean handles deductive proofs; nothing exists for *proof strategies* (meta-reasoning).
- Without formal validation of strategies, RL can't train on strategy quality.
- Tao's wish: a semiformal language for scientific reasoning, analogous to what Lean is for proofs.

## Data-driven conjectures (Gauss example)
- Gauss computed 100,000 primes empirically, found density ≈ x / ln(x) — first major
  data-driven conjecture. No proof; launched analytic number theory.
- Twin prime conjecture: unproved but universally believed because probabilistic prime model
  predicts it. Riemann Hypothesis failure would invalidate the model and threaten prime-based
  cryptography.

## Human moats
- Exposition and persuasion: Darwin's plain English spread ideas in years; Newton's Latin took
  decades. Building a compelling scientific narrative is hard to automate.
- Deep obsessive learning: Tao learns by pursuing things he *should* understand until he can
  explain them himself — AI accelerates this significantly.

## Vibe coding vs. vibe researching
- In software, output (working code) is the goal. In research, understanding gained in the
  process is much of the value.
- AI-generated proofs can still yield insight through post-processing, but this is nascent.

## Tao's current AI usage
- Literature surveys, proof checking, code for numerical experiments, strategy critique.
- Identifies as a "fox" (broad) who collaborates with "hedgehog" deep experts.
