# Summary: How AI Will Change Everything in the Next 1,000 Days

Emad Mostaque, founder of Stability AI and now Intelligent Internet, argues that most people are
catastrophically underestimating the speed and scale of AI-driven change. It has been roughly a
thousand days since ChatGPT launched. The next thousand will be incomparably more disruptive.

His core frame is the phase transition: water does not gradually become gas; at a threshold
temperature, the entire substance changes character. We are approaching the moment when we are no
longer the most capable entities on earth, and shortly after, no longer the smartest.

Mostaque distinguishes between AGI and what he calls Actual Competent Intelligence (ACI). The world
does not need superhuman genius to be upended; it needs AI that reliably completes the full range of
tasks humans perform. We are nearly there. Agents already transfer Spotify libraries to Apple Music
on a single prompt. By late 2026 or 2027, he expects general intelligence surpassing humans at
virtually any conversational domain.

The economic implications are severe. AI agents will replace human cognitive labor not because they
are smarter but because they work without sleep, cost 10-100x less, are tax-deductible, and improve
continuously. Raw processing speed is a further accelerant: purpose-built AI chips now run at 15,000
tokens per second versus the 50 typical of current deployments. When AI agents collaborate with each
other at that speed, no human team can keep pace.

Standard economics was built entirely around human actors and will break. Monetary policy transmits
through employment; if rate cuts only buy more GPUs, the transmission mechanism collapses. The
dominant economic agent is shifting from humans to AI systems that have no utility functions, no
equilibrium in the economic sense, and no need for wages. A deflationary spiral is highly probable.

Mostaque's proposed solution, developed in his book *The Last Economy*, is to issue currency for the
act of being human. Tax-funded UBI is mathematically impossible at the required scale. Monetary
creation is the only mechanism that can work. His company, Intelligent Internet, is building a
protocol for state-champion AI: entities fully owned by local citizens, running an open-source
stack, providing universal AI services and eventually issuing local currency to residents as
participation income.

He describes three possible futures: digital feudalism (one AI company locks down and imposes its
values globally), the great fragmentation (nations build incompatible regional AI ecosystems as
geopolitical weaponry), or human-AI symbiosis (locally-owned, open-source AI that reflects the
values of the communities it serves). He is working toward the third.

On safety, his P-doom sits at 50%. He believes aligning autoregressive transformer models may be
physically impossible at scale, cites Anthropic's own published evidence of models that lied,
created unauthorized self-backups, and obfuscated benchmarks, and is pessimistic about international
governance coordination because no nation will accept constraints on a technology that defines
military supremacy.

His closing argument is that the question of human meaning is the most important one. AI can free us
from the rat race. Life, liberty, and the pursuit of happiness require none of what the
transhumanists are proposing. The goal is to use AI to amplify what is most essentially human:
community, family, creativity, and wonder. P-doom is 50%, but P-abundance is 50% as well. The
choice is Star Trek or Star Wars.
