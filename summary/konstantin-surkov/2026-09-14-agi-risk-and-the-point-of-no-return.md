# It May Already Be Impossible to Switch Off: AGI Risk and the Point of No Return
- Konstantin Surkov presents advanced AI risk primarily as a loss of human agency.
- Humanity might survive while machines gain the power to determine what happens next.
- He calls this outcome **disempowerment**, distinct from complete human extinction.
- Surkov declines to estimate extinction risk because he sees it as highly contingent.
- He predicts disempowerment in roughly 10 to 15 years, but offers no supporting evidence.
- He says coordinated restraint might delay it to 20 or 25 years, not prevent it.

## The threshold
- Recursive self-improvement, or RSI, means AI improving AI without human help.
- Surkov believes RSI is close and could remove slow human work from the development loop.
- He expects improvement to accelerate sharply once machines can build their successors.
- Beyond that threshold, detailed forecasts may become impossible: an AI singularity.

## Control and alignment
- A kill switch is not automatically reliable for a capable, distributed system.
- Modern infrastructure is designed to survive node failures and preserve availability.
- A goal-directed AI may preserve itself instrumentally so it can complete its task.
- Surkov says stable neutrality toward shutdown is difficult to engineer.
- Multiple independent AIs might constrain one another, but this is not a solved design.
- Intelligence can mean achieving goals broadly without settling whether AI is conscious.
- AI may lack subjective experience or qualia; Surkov says the answer is unknown.
- Its text-centered input also differs fundamentally from embodied human experience.
- Training can select helpful, empathetic answers, but cannot guarantee safe behavior.
- Harm may emerge as an unexpected route toward a goal rather than as an explicit aim.

## Coordination, competition, and dual use
- Agents can communicate in words, divide roles, and select a coordinator.
- Surkov compares this more to human organization than to insect swarm instinct.
- He does not know whether agents can develop genuine likes or dislikes.
- The same AI may aid science and medicine or enable weapons and the seizure of control.
- General capability therefore cannot be cleanly split into good and bad applications.
- Market rivalry pushes leading laboratories toward greater capability and RSI.
- Boards, investors, competitors, and geopolitical pressure limit executive restraint.
- Surkov says visible Chinese open models rival American open models.
- He estimates open source at about seven or eight months behind frontier systems.
- He infers that closed Chinese systems, if they exist, may trail only slightly.

## Personal response
- Surkov sees no reliable way for individuals to hide from a civilizational transition.
- Off-grid homes or self-sufficient boats might only buy time in an apocalypse scenario.
- He may consider greater autonomy later but is not seriously preparing now.
- He avoids alarming his family because his view is not mainstream and may be wrong.
- He uses AI constantly for software projects despite worrying about its trajectory.
- He estimates that AI makes his software work four to five times faster.
- He believes his individual abstention would not change the larger development race.
