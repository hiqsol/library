# Nuclear Facilities Under Attack — Summary

[article](../../articles/vitaly-portnikov/2026-03-21-nuclear-facilities-under-attack.md)

Portnikov opens on day 22 of the US-Israel war against Iran with two new alarming
developments just before broadcast.

**Strike on Dimona:** Iranian missiles penetrated Israel's air defense and struck
residential neighborhoods of Dimona, site of Israel's nuclear reactor. Iran says it
targeted workers at the facility; Israel has never officially confirmed it possesses
nuclear weapons. The attack is Iran's retaliation for Israel's second strike on
the Natanz nuclear site, where uranium for a possible Iranian bomb may be stored.

**Diego Garcia missile:** Iran fired two missiles at the US military base on Diego
Garcia island in the Indian Ocean — a flight distance of 4,000 km. Israel's General
Staff confirmed the incident (one was intercepted, one struck). This range means all
of Europe is now within Iranian missile reach, including Ukraine and Poland. The
missiles may be of Russian or Chinese origin, supplied via the Tajik-Iranian border
where Russia maintains a military division.

**Iran's strategic logic:** Iran is a revolutionary state seeking to export Islamic
theocracy across the Middle East. It passed a law mandating Israel's destruction and
built the Axis of Resistance (Hamas, Hezbollah, Iraqi and Yemeni militias) to execute
it. Iran accelerated its nuclear program after observing that nuclear-armed Russia
invaded Ukraine with impunity while the West could only watch. A nuclear bomb would
give Iran the same protection.

**US-Israel campaign:** The US and Israel have destroyed Iran's navy, most of its air
force, and can eliminate most nuclear facilities. However, a full destruction of Iran's
missile arsenal and regime change require a ground operation — potentially costing
tens of thousands of American lives — which Trump cannot politically survive. His MAGA
base voted against new wars; daily military funeral footage would trigger Republican
impeachment proceedings.

**Leadership vacuum in Iran:** Supreme Leader Khamenei has not appeared in public
since US-Israeli strikes. His Nowruz greetings were posted on Telegram without video.
President Pezeshkian issues statements but holds no constitutional command authority
and contradicts himself (apologizes for Arab strikes, then strikes intensify). It is
unclear who actually controls Iran's military.

**Negotiations impasse:** Trump wants Iranian nuclear disarmament, end to missile
program, and no threats to Israel or Gulf states. Iran demands a full nuclear cycle
and reparations. No basis for a quick deal exists. Trump's planned China visit was
cancelled; Xi Jinping will wait for Trump's economic exhaustion to dictate terms.

**Regional diplomacy:** Turkish FM Hakan Fidan proposed to Iran: end hostilities,
provide Gulf states with security guarantees, and in exchange receive economic
cooperation. Gulf states warned that continued Iranian strikes on their territory will
require countermeasures. Saudi Arabia immediately ordered Iranian embassy staff out
within 24 hours. Saudi Arabia may also withdraw its financial payments to Pakistan
after Islamabad refused to honor its mutual defense agreement.

**Article 5 parallel:** Pakistan's non-intervention illustrates that alliance
commitments dissolve when they become costly. NATO Article 5 would likely not be
invoked in a Russian hybrid operation against Narva, Estonia — Russia would use
"separatists" without regular troops, and the West would call for consultations, not
collective defense. Putin cannot open a second front while the Ukraine war is active.

**Russia's oil windfall:** Trump partially suspended Russia sanctions for the duration
of the Iran war. If maintained several more months, Russia stands to earn an extra
$250 billion from oil sales to fund its war against Ukraine. A Ukrainian delegation is
in Washington meeting Witkoff and Kushner to address this.

**Ukraine dimension:** Iran enabled Russia's war with Shahed drone deliveries and
factory design assistance; Iranian drones fly over Ukrainian cities in real time.
Iran has explicitly threatened Ukraine with missile strikes. The Iranian embassy in
Kyiv remains open, which Portnikov considers indefensible. Ukraine's drone/missile
defense specialists are consulting the US and Gulf states. The Iran war outcome
directly determines whether Ukraine survives as a state.

**European defense:** A European Defense Union is unlikely while the US nuclear
umbrella exists. Pro-Trump European politicians are blocking it — illustrated by Polish
President Nawrocki's veto on Poland's EU rearmament participation. If Ukraine ends
the war with a neutrality clause, any future alliance membership is a post-2030s
question. The immediate goal is surviving as a sovereign state, not as Russian oblasts.
