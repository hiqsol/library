# "Portnikov: US Has Lost the War in Iran — Catastrophe With Massive

[article](../../articles/vitaly-portnikov/2026-03-26-us-lost-iran-war-trump-crisis.md)

Portnikov argues the US has no good options in Iran after Trump
extended his ultimatum by five days. Three scenarios — negotiated
coexistence, continued air strikes, or a ground invasion — all carry
severe risks ranging from energy catastrophe to tens of thousands
of American casualties.

Trump's inner circle is divided into political careerists (Vance,
Rubio), appointees without independent standing (Hegseth), and the
core financial group (Kushner, Witkoff, Trump family) that treats
the presidency as a vehicle for speculative enrichment. Trump's
"simulated madness" — a lifelong business tactic — creates market
swings that benefit insiders, but his deteriorating psychological
state blurs the line between strategy and instability.

Iran holds the initiative because the Supreme Leader, the only one
who can order all armed forces, refuses to engage with Trump.
Each day of resistance deepens the global energy crisis. If the US
withdraws without regime change, the geopolitical fallout mirrors
the Vietnam War defeat — but this time adversaries have functioning
economies.

Portnikov draws a parallel between Iran's resistance and Ukraine's
stand against Russia: both invaders expected quick capitulation.
The key difference is that Iran, unlike democratic Ukraine, strikes
at third-party infrastructure (Gulf states), making it far more
dangerous for the US.

On Israel, Portnikov calls Netanyahu's belief that air strikes
alone could topple Iran a catastrophic loss of competence. Israel's
political elite has eroded since the generation of Sharon and Peres
cleared the field around them.

On Ukraine, Portnikov notes Zelenskyy's personalist governance
began degrading state institutions in 2019, before the war. A
national unity government was needed but never formed. Meanwhile,
Orban is a real problem — blocking 90 billion euros in EU funds
and reportedly leaking EU deliberations on Ukraine to Moscow.

Key insight: the US has not won a single full-scale war since WWII.
It wins battles but loses wars, and Iran may be the conflict that
produces a definitive geopolitical defeat.
