Vitaly Portnikov on Pryamiy's "Devick" program (March 7, 2026) with
Peter Zalmayev analyzes the US-Israeli war against Iran, its impact on
Ukraine, and Hungary's pre-election provocations.

## Hungary exploits Ukraine before elections

- Orban deliberately injects the Ukraine factor into Hungary's
  pre-election campaign to deflect from poor economic performance,
  reprising his "prime minister of peace" strategy from prior elections
- FM Szijjarto visited Moscow; Putin promised stable oil/gas prices and
  released two Ukrainian POWs with Hungarian citizenship
- Hungarian anti-terror units seized two Ukrainian cash transport
  vehicles ($40M), framed as "corruption money"
- Former FM Kuleba describes Druzhba pipeline as a dead end: restoring
  flow helps Orban, not restoring it also helps him
- Portnikov warns the global energy crisis may force the EU to demand
  Ukraine restore Druzhba, rendering the current standoff trivial

## Iran war: strategic miscalculation

- Trump and Netanyahu expected initial strikes to trigger regime-changing
  uprising — Portnikov warned this was illusory
- Regimes cannot be toppled from the air alone — a proven military truth
- White House formula: operation ends when Trump personally decides
  capitulation occurred; Iran's acknowledgment is irrelevant
- Economic trap: Hormuz Strait closed, gas prices rising, job growth
  stalled; continued war risks domestic crisis that could end Trump's
  career and enable Democratic impeachment if they win both chambers
- NYT reconstruction: Trump hesitated but was persuaded by Netanyahu and
  Senator Lindsey Graham

## Antisemitism paradox in MAGA coalition

- Trump leads a coalition with prominent antisemitic forces (Carlson
  expelled, MTG statements) yet personally prioritizes Israel's security
- This mirrors Biden's trap: his Israel support alienated ultra-left
  voters who abstained in 2024, helping Trump win
- For Israel, Trump may be the last pro-Israel US president — polling
  shows majority of Americans now favor Palestinian perspective

## Ukraine gains leverage through expertise

- US military consulting Ukraine on countering Iranian drones —
  Americans proved unprepared for modern warfare despite 4 years of
  observing the Russia-Ukraine conflict
- US spending millions per interceptor on cheap mass-produced drones
- Ukraine's army is the only force with real drone warfare experience,
  creating leverage with both the US and Europe

## Russia: the real beneficiary

- US Treasury Secretary Bessent announced partial lifting of Russian
  oil sanctions to prevent energy chaos
- India returning to Russian oil; EU faces dilemma on Indian-refined
  Russian petroleum products
- German intelligence: Russia's budget deficit is 1/3 higher than
  official figures due to sanctions
- But every day of Iran war gives Putin more resources for the Ukraine
  war in the 2020s-2030s
- Portnikov: Russia is a regional power, not geopolitical — losses in
  Syria, Venezuela, Iran are irrelevant; what matters is money for war

## Russia aids Iran while US looks away

- Washington Post and Radio Liberty report Russia helping Iran with
  targeting against US objectives
- Trump refused to comment; US abstained from IAEA vote on Russian
  attacks on Ukrainian civilians
- Portnikov: Trump cannot abandon his myth of good Putin relations;
  accepting the intelligence would elevate Russia to peer competitor
