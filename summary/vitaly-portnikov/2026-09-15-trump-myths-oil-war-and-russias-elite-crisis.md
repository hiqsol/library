## Energy, fuel prices, and elections
- Trump claimed an energy ceasefire, but the host said there was no confirmation.
- Portnikov read the claim as messaging before the November 3 US midterms.
- He cited gasoline near $6 a gallon and discussion of a possible $10 price.
- Trump officials blamed Ukrainian refinery strikes and lost Russian diesel supply.
- Portnikov said the Persian Gulf diesel loss was at least twice the Russian loss.
- He tied the larger shock to Hormuz and possible disruption at Bab el-Mandeb.
- He expected no near-term truce because Putin valued continuing the war more.

## The Trump wedding allegation
- The host cited a report about Kremlev helping fund Donald Trump Jr.'s wedding.
- The reported fireworks and other support totaled several hundred thousand dollars.
- Portnikov described Kremlev as Kremlin-linked; that characterization is attributed.
- He suggested a costly gift could be an entry ticket to later business cooperation.
- He did not establish whether Trump Jr.'s anti-Ukraine views had a financial motive.
- He said Democratic control of the House could produce broader investigations.

## Putin's business-network pattern
- Portnikov framed Putin less as a master spy than as a St. Petersburg dealmaker.
- He cited footage from August 23 or 24, 1991, with Putin beside Anatoly Sobchak.
- About 35 years later, he saw the same pitch operating through intermediaries.
- He speculated about gray deals, hidden income, greed, and promises of quick victory.

## Ukraine's prosecutor-general and NABU

## Trains, Western visitors, and NATO spillover
